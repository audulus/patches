MIDI Input

Play an Audulus instrument from a MIDI keyboard or sequencer. Provides pitch and velocity-sensitive gates, with selectable polyphony and pitch bend range.

Controls

  • Voice mode: Legato, Poly 2, Poly 4, Poly 8, or Poly 16. Match your instrument’s voice count to the selected mode.
  • Pitchbend: sets the bend range from ±1 to ±12 semitones. Match this to the range expected by your instrument or performance.

Outputs

  • Pitch: connect to an oscillator’s octave/pitch input. Uses 1 unit per octave, with 0 at A4 (440 Hz). Pitch bend is summed with signal.
  • Gate: connect to an envelope’s gate input. Gate height follows note velocity; polyphonic modes provide a pitch and gate for each voice.

Notes

Legato is for single-note playing; polyphonic modes allow chords. Route the intended MIDI source into Audulus. The pitch-bend section responds on all MIDI channels and bends every active voice together.
