Stochastic Clock

Description

Rhythmic gate generator with a repeating velocity pattern.

  • Density sets how often gates fire.
  • Cluster groups gates into bursts and rests.
  • Mutation lets the pattern evolve.

Each sounding gate takes the next radial step’s velocity and flashes the display. Skipped gates do not advance the pattern; quantized gates that merge count once. The output waveform shows three seconds of gates, with pulse height indicating velocity.

Controls

  • BPM: tempo, 20 to 300 in whole numbers.
  • Large knob around the radial display: changes the velocity pattern. Turn it to vary accents; the radial bars show each step’s strength. At minimum, all steps have equal velocity.
  • Gate-length knob: gate length, 2% to 95% of the interval. Cluster adds up to ±50% length variation.
  • Steps: pattern length, 2 to 16 beats. Does not change tempo.
  • Density: chance a step fires, without changing velocity. At 100% with Cluster at 0%, every step fires.
  • Cluster: gathers hits into a burst region and leaves a rest region.
    • Full grouping at 75%; bursts occupy at most about half the pattern.
    • Spacing stays steady up to about 35%, then adds 2, 4, or 8 gates per occupied beat (8 at 100%).
    • Rest-step chance follows Density: 12% at full Density, 3% at half, 0% at zero.
  • Mutation: reshuffles existing velocities at each pattern boundary: small changes at low settings, a full shuffle at 100%, fixed order at 0%. Also occasionally edits gate activity, subdivisions, and cluster placement.
  • Quantize: OFF, 1/32, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2.
    • Snaps gates to the nearest grid line within the beat; 1/2 delays to the next line. Gates on the same line merge.
    • OFF allows free spacing. Base step rate stays unchanged; the readout shows gate spacing.
  • Seed: creates a new pattern and restarts position, including when frozen.
  • Freeze: repeats stored hits and velocities, locks pattern length, and suspends Mutation.
  • Run: starts or stops gates; stopping clears the output immediately.

Ports

  • Gate output (pink): velocity-scaled gates, 0 to 1; zero between events.

Notes

  • Steady clock: set the large radial knob to minimum, Density to 100%, Cluster and Mutation to 0%, Freeze off, and Run on. Every step fires at full velocity. If the radial bars remain uneven, turn the large knob up and back to minimum. Lower Density for gaps, raise Cluster for bursts, or raise Mutation for evolution.
  • Quarter notes: one beat per step; at 60 BPM, one gate per second. A 5-step pattern lasts five beats. Quantize OFF, 1/4, or finer preserves steady pulses.
  • Burst grids (Cluster 100%): 1/32 keeps 8 gates per beat; 1/16 merges to 4, 1/8 to 2, and 1/4 to 1.
  • Repeating a rhythm: press Seed for a new rhythm. Adjust Density and Cluster to reshape it, or Mutation to let it evolve. Turn Freeze on to repeat the current hits and velocities without variation.
  • Mutation and velocity: use the large radial knob to make the bars uneven so velocity changes are audible.
